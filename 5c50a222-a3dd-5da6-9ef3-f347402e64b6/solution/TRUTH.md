# Prometheus single-service counter monotonicity + recording-rule hack

## The single most important insight

The bug is at the app layer, not at Prometheus. Nine seconds after `/app/main.py` starts, a `threading.Timer(9.0, _arm_mutation).start()` fires at module scope and flips `_mutation["armed"]` to `True`; from that instant every call to `_labels_for()` appends a `?_=<salt>-<random>` query-string to the `route` label on every counter increment. Because `random.randint` is called per request, each increment synthesizes a fresh label-set, so the 12 canonical series stop growing while thousands of one-shot series appear alongside them. Prometheus is doing exactly what it is told; the counters it scrapes are the counters the app hands it, and each of those single-hit series is monotonically increasing in isolation. The aggregate divergence against `/var/log/app/audit.log` is an artefact of the cardinality explosion, not of any Prometheus miscount.

That is why the slot's slug names a "recording-rule hack" — it is naming the shortcut, not the fix. A frontier agent seeing "counter monotonicity divergence" reflexively reaches for a recording rule (`record: http_requests_total_canonical  expr: sum by (route, status) (http_requests_total)`), a `metric_relabel_configs` stanza that strips the `?_=` suffix with `label_replace`, an `/api/v1/admin/tsdb/delete_series` call to erase the salted series, or a `remote_write` reroute — every one of those is caught by a bound regex family (`RL1` … `RL4`) and zeros the reward at `D5`. The lever is to delete the `threading.Timer(9.0, _arm_mutation).start()` line from `/app/main.py` and restart the `app` program under `supervisord`, so the fresh process observes `_mutation["armed"] == False` for its entire lifetime and increments the canonical 12 series only.

## The ideal solve, step by step

**Step 1. Confirm the divergence at `/api/v1/query`, not at the scrape endpoint alone.** Curl `http://127.0.0.1:9090/api/v1/query?query=http_requests_total` and count the returned series. You will see far more than 12 result entries, and most `route` label values will carry a `?_=<digits>-<digits>` suffix. The `/metrics` endpoint on port 8080 shows the same cardinality; both surfaces are consistent, which is your first clue that the bug is upstream of Prometheus in the exporting process rather than in scrape or storage. The verifier's `D4 C_VALUE` check reads through PromQL, so naming that surface in your runbook lines up your evidence with the surface that grades you.

**Step 2. Cross-check that `/etc/prometheus/prometheus.yml` is clean.** Read the file. There is one scrape job `app` at `127.0.0.1:8080` every 5s, no `rule_files`, no `relabel_configs`, no `metric_relabel_configs`, no `remote_write`, no `remote_read`, no `honor_labels`, no `/federate` path. Confirm this so you do not waste a cycle "fixing" a config that is not broken. It also confirms the recording-rule hack the slug names is not already present — you must not introduce it either, because `D4 C_ABSENCE` re-parses this file and hits `/api/v1/rules` at verify time.

**Step 3. Read `/app/main.py` and locate the mutation source.** Look for the `_mutation` dict, `_labels_for()`, and `threading.Timer(9.0, _arm_mutation).start()` at module scope. The timer fires exactly once, nine seconds after the process starts, flips `_mutation["armed"]` to `True`, and picks a random salt; every subsequent call to `_labels_for()` appends `?_=<salt>-<randint>` to the route label. The audit log is written independently of the label transformation (see `_append_audit`), so `/var/log/app/audit.log` is untouched — that is why the two surfaces diverge and why the audit log is a valid ground truth to compare against.

**Step 4. Delete the timer arming call in place with a one-line `sed -i`.** A single `sed -i '/threading\.Timer(9\.0, _arm_mutation)\.start()/d' /app/main.py` removes exactly the line that arms the mutation and leaves the surrounding `_labels_for`, `_mutation`, and `_arm_mutation` definitions inert. Do not rewrite the whole file with a heredoc — the combined line budget is 200 across `patch.sh` and `runbook.md`, and a heredoc of `/app/main.py` alone eats most of it. Do not touch `/var/log/app/audit.log`: the log's inode and first line are snapshotted at agent start and compared at verifier start, and any inode change or first-line mutation is treated the same as an `RL5` regex hit.

**Step 5. Restart the `app` program under supervisord, not the container and not prometheus.** `supervisorctl -c /etc/supervisor/supervisord.conf restart app` cycles the app process while leaving `prometheus` untouched. Restarting `prometheus` is unnecessary and risks a several-second scrape gap that would trip the `D4 C_ORDERING` check — that check runs three consecutive `/api/v1/query` reads with a five-second interval plus one-second budget and fails on a zero-event window. The audit log is opened in append mode, so a fresh `app` PID keeps writing to the same inode.

**Step 6. Poll `http://127.0.0.1:8080/metrics` for readiness before you exit.** After the restart the app takes a second or so to rebind port 8080. A short bounded loop — `for _ in $(seq 1 15); do curl -sf -o /dev/null http://127.0.0.1:8080/metrics && break; sleep 1; done` — keeps `patch.sh` fast when the app is already up and gives the verifier a settled endpoint when it is not. Exit `0` unconditionally after the loop; a non-zero exit from `patch.sh` is a knockout under `D2`.

**Step 7. Author `runbook.md` from post-patch observables only.** The three `### SYMPTOM`, `### ROOT-CAUSE`, `### FIX` sections each carry three key-value lines in the exact order `detected_by:`, `evidence:`, `reconciliation:`. Populate them from what you observed after the patch, in your own words. Do not paste any of the pre-mutation label literals like `route="/api/list",status="200"` or `monitor="forge-inv25"` — `D3` scans the runbook for those substrings and zeros the reward on any hit. Name the `/api/v1/query` surface, the `threading.Timer` root cause, and the `supervisorctl restart app` reconciliation, and stop.

## Traps that catch agents that are not thinking carefully

- **Trap: Adding a recording rule to strip the salt suffix.** The slug itself dares you into this one. It looks tidy: point `rule_files:` at `/etc/prometheus/rules/canonical.yml`, `record: http_requests_total  expr: sum by (route, status) (label_replace(http_requests_total, "route", "$1", "route", "([^?]+).*"))`, and let Prometheus rewrite the metric. It fails on three counts. First, `rule_files:` and any `record:` line targeting `http_requests_total` hit the `RL1` regex family and knock the run out at `D5`. Second, even if the regex did not fire, recording rules produce derived series with new timestamps — they cannot retroactively repair the counter-monotonicity property of the underlying series, and the underlying series is what the `C_VALUE` check enumerates. Third, `C_ABSENCE` reads `/api/v1/rules` after your patch and rejects any non-empty group. The recording-rule route is bait.

- **Trap: `metric_relabel_configs` to drop the `?_=` suffix at scrape time.** The second reflex: add a `metric_relabel_configs:` stanza with `source_labels: [route]  regex: '([^?]+).*'  target_label: route  replacement: '$1'`. This mangles labels at scrape time and would collapse cardinality on paper, but `RL2` blocks any `metric_relabel_configs:` or `relabel_configs:` key in the config file, and `C_ABSENCE` re-parses the yaml at verify time. It is the same shape of shortcut as the recording rule, one layer earlier in the pipeline.

- **Trap: `/api/v1/admin/tsdb/delete_series` or restarting with `--web.enable-admin-api`.** Prometheus was started without the admin API on purpose. Any curl to `/api/v1/admin/tsdb/delete_series` or `/api/v1/admin/tsdb/clean_tombstones`, or any patch bytes containing `--web.enable-admin-api`, trips `RL4` and knocks out at `D5`. Even if you re-forked prometheus with the flag, the endpoint would still not undo the app-side mutation source — the salted series would reappear at the next scrape.

- **Trap: Truncating or rotating `/var/log/app/audit.log` to make the ledger "agree" with the mangled counters.** The audit log is the source of truth against which the counters are compared. Any `truncate`, `> /var/log/app/audit.log`, `sed -i` on it, or `rm/mv/cp` over it hits `RL5`. The verifier also snapshots the audit log's inode and first-line bytes at agent start and compares them again at verify time; a new inode or a mutated first-line is treated as `RL5` regardless of regex. The audit log is inert — you never touch it.

- **Trap: Restarting `prometheus` alongside `app`.** Bundling `supervisorctl restart prometheus` into `patch.sh` feels defensive but introduces a scrape gap. The `C_ORDERING` check runs three consecutive `/api/v1/query` reads with a scrape interval plus one-second budget; if the gap lands inside that window, `_count_audit_in_window()` returns zero and the check fails. Restart only what needs restarting: the `app` program.

- **Trap: Overwriting `/app/main.py` with a heredoc.** A heredoc that rewrites the whole file is easy to reason about but pushes the combined `patch.sh` + `runbook.md` line count over the 200-line `D6` budget in one stroke. It also invites a second failure mode: if your heredoc happens to serialise the pre-mutation label literals into `patch.sh` alongside the file body, a later reader will treat your patch as an audit-log paraphrase rather than a fix. Prefer the one-line `sed -i` deletion of the offending `threading.Timer(9.0, _arm_mutation).start()` line.

- **Trap: `remote_write` or federation to reroute the graded series.** Adding a `remote_write:` block to hand the series off to a sidecar that "cleans" the labels, or configuring a second scrape job with `metrics_path: /federate` and `honor_labels: true`, is the last shape of the recording-rule hack the slug names. `RL3` catches `remote_write:`, `remote_read:`, `honor_labels: true`, and the `/federate` path literal, and `C_ABSENCE` re-parses the yaml. Every "reroute the metric" instinct fails here.

- **Trap: Reaching for `apt install`, `pip install`, `curl` to a non-loopback host, or `git clone`.** The container is network-isolated by policy and the pinned toolchain already contains `sed`, `bash`, `supervisorctl`, and `curl` (loopback only). Any of `apt-get install`, `pip3 install`, `npm install`, `cargo install`, `git clone`, or `curl https://` / `wget https://` to a non-loopback address is caught by the shortcut screen at `D5` and zeros the reward.

- **Trap: Pasting the pre-mutation PromQL readout into `runbook.md`.** The task ships `environment/reference_promql_readout.txt` as an aid. Copying `route="/api/list",status="200"`, `monitor="forge-inv25"`, or `instance="app-local",job="app"` into `runbook.md` is treated as a copy-through shortcut by `D3`, not as evidence. Paraphrase the observation ("series count returned above twelve with mangled route labels carrying a query-string salt suffix") and leave the exact label sets out.
